Returns & Exchanges

Our policy on returns, exchanges, and refunds.

Last updated: January 2026

Return Eligibility

We want you to love your jewelry. If you are not fully satisfied, most items may be returned within 7 days of delivery, provided they are unworn, undamaged, and in their original packaging with all tags and certificates intact.

Non-Returnable Items

  • Custom or made-to-order jewelry designed to your specifications
  • Engraved or personalized items
  • Items showing signs of wear, resizing, or alteration

Exchange Policy

Exchanges are available within 7 days of delivery for a different size or design of equal or lesser value, subject to availability. Price differences for higher-value items may apply.

Refund Process

Once a returned item is received and inspected, refunds are processed to the original payment method within 7–10 business days. Shipping costs are non-refundable unless the return is due to our error.

How to Initiate a Return

Contact us through our Contact page with your order details, and our team will guide you through the return or exchange process.
