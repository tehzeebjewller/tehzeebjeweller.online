Returns & Refund Policy

Last updated: 2025 | Tehzeeb Jeweller

Important Notice

Return and refund eligibility is confirmed at the time of purchase and is specific to each order. Please contact Tehzeeb Jeweller before returning any item to discuss your options and receive authorization.

1. Return Eligibility

Returns are handled on a case-by-case basis depending on:

  • Condition of the jewellery upon receipt
  • Whether the item is custom-made or from our standard collection
  • Time elapsed since purchase
  • Reason for return
  • Your specific order terms and conditions

2. Contacting Us About Returns

If you wish to return an item, please contact us immediately:

WhatsApp: +92 331 6955440

Email: 6955440@gmail.com

3. Condition of Items

Items must be returned in original condition with all packaging and documentation. Items that show signs of wear, damage, or alterations may not be eligible for return or refund.

4. Customized Jewellery

Custom-made jewellery pieces, once completed and confirmed, are generally not returnable or refundable as they are created specifically to your specifications. Returns of custom pieces are considered only if there are defects in craftsmanship.

5. Damaged Items

If you receive damaged jewellery, please notify us immediately with photographs of the damage. We will assess the situation and discuss replacement or refund options with you.

6. Exchanges

Exchanges for different sizes, designs, or styles may be available depending on availability and your order terms. Contact us to discuss exchange possibilities.

7. Refund Process

If a refund is approved:

  • We will confirm the refund amount and method
  • Refunds are processed to the original payment method
  • Processing time varies depending on your payment method and financial institution
  • International refunds may incur banking fees

8. Shipping Costs

Shipping costs for returns are typically the responsibility of the customer unless the return is due to our error or a defective item. Please confirm shipping arrangements with us before returning any item.

9. Order Cancellation

If you wish to cancel an order before it is completed or shipped, please contact us as soon as possible. Cancellation eligibility depends on the order status and your original terms.

10. International Returns

For customers in the USA, UK, Canada, Dubai, or other international locations, return and refund policies are subject to additional considerations including customs, import duties, and international shipping. Please discuss these factors with our team before initiating a return.

11. Timeline

Return requests should be initiated within a reasonable timeframe after purchase. Our team will work with you to find the best resolution based on your situation.
